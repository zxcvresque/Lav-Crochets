Policy · cookie statement

Cookies.

Two functional cookies. No tracking. No third-party analytics.

This site sets exactly two cookies. Both are essential for the shop to work; neither is for advertising or analytics.

NamePurposeDurationType
jw_session Keeps you signed in. Without this cookie, the magic-link flow does not work. 30 days Essential
jw_currency Remembers your display-currency override from the footer switcher. 1 year Preference

What we don't set

  • Google Analytics cookies
  • Facebook Pixel cookies
  • Ad-targeting cookies of any kind
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Affiliate-attribution cookies

Third-party cookies

When you go to the Dodo Payments checkout, Dodo sets its own cookies for fraud prevention. Those are governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

How to opt out

You can block cookies for joywavedigital.shop in your browser. If you do, you will need to sign in via magic link every time and you will lose your currency override. Receipts still work; everything that doesn't require a session still works.
