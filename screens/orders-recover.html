Lost the email? No problem.

Enter the address you used at checkout. We will look up any orders attached to it and resend the receipt with fresh download links. The new links are valid for 14 days.

Recover by email

For security, we send the same neutral response whether or not we find a match. Check your inbox in the next minute.

Check your inbox.

If we have any orders for that address, a recovery email is on its way. If nothing arrives within 5 minutes, the address may not match any orders, or it may have been mistyped. Try again or contact support.

Used a different email?

People often pay with a personal Gmail and use a school address at work. Try each address you might have used. If nothing matches, email hello@joywavedigital.shop from the address attached to the order and we can resend manually.
