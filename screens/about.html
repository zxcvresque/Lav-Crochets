About

A shop for the adult doing
the buying.

JoyWaveDigital is a one-person shop. I make educational printables for ages 3 to 12, and I make them for the parent, teacher, or homeschooler holding the credit card. The kid is the user; the adult is the buyer. Both should feel respected.

Why I started this shop.

I ran an Etsy shop for seven months. Three hundred and sixteen orders. Worksheets, decodable readers, classroom labels. The customers were good; the platform was not. Etsy's chrome talks to the wrong person, the fees move the wrong way, and the rules change without notice.

So I built a home for the printables. Same catalogue, better surface. Etsy stays open for the next twelve months as a fallback. This site is the front door.

Three rules

What I will not do.

The category default for educational printables is cute, saturated, sticker-heavy, infantilising. I reject all of it.

  1. 01No Comic Sans.
  2. 02No clip art. Real typography or nothing.
  3. 03No infantilising the buyer. The kid still gets to be a kid.

How I work.

Every printable goes through a real classroom. A friend teaches grade 2 in Pune; another runs an after-school programme in Mumbai. I send drafts, they hand them to actual kids, and we revise. Worksheets that do not get used get rewritten or shelved.

The art uses real typography (Bricolage Grotesque for display, a serif body for trace-and-print work) and prints clean at 600 dpi. Black-and-white-friendly so school printers handle them. Editable answer keys, because graders are human and answer keys lose pages.

Three things I commit to:

  • Instant download. Receipts arrive in under 30 seconds. If they don't, the recovery form is one tap from the footer.
  • No account on first buy. You pay with an email; the files come to that email. Make a library later if you want one.
  • Refunds, no debate. Fourteen days, any reason, same-day refund. If a printable is not what you expected, that is feedback I want.
