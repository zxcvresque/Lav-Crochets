Policy · accessibility statement

Accessibility.

Targeting WCAG 2.2 AA across the storefront. Known gaps surfaced below.

What we hold to

  • Body text contrast at or above 4.5:1; large text at or above 3:1; non-text UI at or above 3:1.
  • Every interactive element keyboard reachable.
  • Focus-visible outlines on every interactive element.
  • Touch targets at or above 44 by 44 pixels.
  • Reduced-motion fallback for every animation that respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Skip-to-content link on every page.
  • Form labels associated with inputs; error messages announced via aria-live.
  • All non-decorative images have alt text.
  • Site navigable end-to-end without a mouse.

Known gaps

  • The product cluster on the home hero is decorative; it carries aria-hidden="true" so it does not pollute the screen-reader pass.
  • Tiptap rich-text content in product descriptions inherits from the editor's defaults. Reading order is correct but heading levels in author-provided content depend on the founder.

Reduced motion

The site respects your OS setting. You can also force it on per-account in settings. When on, the following animations are disabled or replaced with opacity fades:

  • Header pill-on-scroll transition (snaps instead of slides)
  • Card hover lift and asymmetric expansion
  • Eyebrow pulse
  • Cart drawer slide (fades instead)
  • Heart badge-pop

Browsers + assistive tech tested

  • VoiceOver on macOS Safari (Sequoia)
  • VoiceOver on iOS Safari (iOS 18)
  • NVDA on Windows Firefox + Chrome
  • Keyboard-only navigation in Chrome, Safari, Firefox

Report a barrier

If something on this site stops you from doing what you came to do, please tell us. Email accessibility@joywavedigital.shop with what happened and what device you were using. We aim to fix accessibility regressions within 14 days.
