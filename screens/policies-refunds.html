Refund policy.

Fourteen days, any reason, same-day refund.

Plain version, no fine print:

  • If you bought a printable from us and you do not want it, reply to your receipt within 14 days. We refund.
  • We do not ask why. If you want to share what was not right, we use the feedback to write better product descriptions, but it is not required.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually settle within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Once refunded, your download links are revoked. Please delete the files; they are no longer licensed to you.

How to request

  1. Reply to the receipt email with the word "refund". One word is enough.
  2. Or use the contact form with the order number.
  3. Or email hello@joywavedigital.shop directly.

We typically process refunds within a few hours, weekdays Mumbai time.

Partial refunds

If you want some items but not others, ask for a partial. We refund the items you do not want and keep the rest available in your library.

Special cases

Wrong file delivered

If we sent you a different file than what was on the listing, we refund and send the correct file at no charge. Email with the order number and what you actually got.

Download failed

If the file is corrupted or will not open, we do not require a refund. Use the receipt-recovery form for a fresh link. If a fresh link still fails, email and we will send the file directly.

Volume / wholesale orders

Volume orders (10+ classroom licences) are refundable within 14 days only if the files have not been distributed beyond the original buyer. After distribution, partial refunds may apply.
