Privacy.

We collect what we need to ship printables to you. Nothing more, no exceptions.

What we collect

  • Email. To send receipts and download links.
  • Order details. What you bought, when, in what currency. Required for tax records.
  • Payment metadata. The last four digits of your card and the network (Visa, Mastercard). We never see or store your full card number; Dodo Payments holds it.
  • Wishlist + library state. Only if you make an account.
  • Server logs. IP address and user agent for 30 days, for security and abuse prevention only.

What we never collect

  • Your name (unless you tell us in a message)
  • Your address (we sell digital files; there is nothing to ship)
  • Your child's information
  • Cross-site tracking data
  • Marketing-pixel data from third parties

What we never sell

Your email, your order history, your IP address, your behavior. We do not have advertising partners. We do not have data brokers. The shop makes money by selling printables.

Who else sees your data

Three vendors process your data on our behalf:

  • Dodo Payments: processes your card payment. Their privacy policy applies to the card details we never see.
  • Supabase: hosts our database and file storage. Hosted in Singapore.
  • Resend: sends transactional emails (receipts, magic links).

That is the whole list. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, TikTok pixel, or any other tracking SDK.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and DPDPA you have the right to:

  • Access your data (export available in settings)
  • Correct it (edit in profile)
  • Delete it (delete-account flow in profile; we keep order records 7 years for tax law, everything else goes within 30 days)
  • Object to processing (turn off marketing in settings; transactional emails are required for receipts)
  • Data portability (JSON export in settings)

Cookies

See the cookie policy. Short version: we use one functional cookie for auth and one preference cookie for currency. No tracking cookies.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@joywavedigital.shop. Replies within 72 hours.
