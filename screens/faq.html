FAQ.

Twelve questions I get every week. If yours is not here, email.

  • How do downloads work?

    Pay, get an email with a button labelled "download". Tap it. The PDF or ZIP arrives. The link is good for 14 days; refresh with the receipt-recovery form if it expires.

  • Do I need to make an account?

    No. The first purchase happens with just an email. Make an account later if you want a library you can come back to, sale alerts on wishlisted items, or order history.

  • What are these printables licensed for?

    One classroom per purchase. Print as many copies as your kids need within that room. If you teach multiple classes or run a school, email for a volume licence; starts at 10 seats.

  • What file format do they come in?

    PDF for most worksheets and posters. ZIP for packs and bundles. Every product card lists the format and page count up front; no surprises.

  • Can I print in black and white?

    Yes. Every printable is designed to read clearly on a black-and-white school printer. Colour is decorative; the pedagogy survives a grayscale photocopy.

  • What is your refund policy?

    Fourteen days, any reason, same-day refund. Reply to your receipt email or use the contact form. We do not ask why, but if you tell us, we use the feedback to write better product descriptions.

  • What currency do you charge in?

    USD. We display local currency on cards and detail surfaces so you know what you are paying; the actual charge is in USD. Your card issuer handles the conversion.

  • Do you do free samples?

    Yes. The freebies page has the current free pack and a few standalone free printables. New free pack every quarter.

  • How often do you ship new printables?

    One new product a week, usually on Mondays. Sometimes a small one mid-week. The monthly letter recaps everything; subscribe in settings.

  • I bought from your Etsy shop. Can I move my orders here?

    Yes, eventually. The migration tool is in build; sign up for the monthly letter and you will hear when it ships. In the meantime, the Etsy shop stays open as a fallback for at least 12 months.

  • Where are you based?

    Mumbai, India. Sold globally. One-person shop, no warehouse, no co-founders. Replies are usually within a few hours, sometimes faster.

  • Can I license a printable for a YouTube video, blog post, or app?

    Email me. The classroom licence does not cover redistribution; commercial licences exist for everything in the catalogue, priced case by case.
