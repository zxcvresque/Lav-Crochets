How this shop works

  • 01
    Drawn, set, and proofed by me.

    Every page in the shop went through one pair of hands. I do not use template generators.

  • 02
    Instant download. PDF and PNG.

    You get both formats. Use the PDF to print. Use the PNGs in a slide deck or a class app.

  • 03
    If something breaks, email me.

    I read every email myself. Most replies happen within a working day.
