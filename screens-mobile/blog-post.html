Why I color-coded the 100s chart by ten

[ feature image ]

The first version was monochrome. Black lines, white background, even spacing. I thought it was clean.

My kid did not finish a row. She would lose her place after 14 and start counting again from 1.

Version two used ten colors, one per row. Pink for 1 to 10, peach for 11 to 20, butter yellow for 21 to 30. She got to 100 in one sitting.

What changed

The colors gave each ten a place. She could look at any row and know how far in she was without counting back to 1. The math system stopped being one long line; it became ten neighborhoods.

What I would do differently

Pick warmer colors next time. The teal I used for 41 to 50 reads cold next to peach; she skipped that row twice.
