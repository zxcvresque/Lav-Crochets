What I will and will not refund

Refunds

Plain language. If something here is unclear, email me.

Last updated Apr 2026

The short version

Digital files do not auto-qualify for blanket refunds. But if a file does not work, I will refund you. Always.

When yes

Wrong file delivered. Corrupt or unprintable PDF. Duplicate accidental purchase. Wrong currency charged.

When no

Change of mind after download. Wrong format expectations (it says PDF on the page; it ships as PDF).

How

Email me with the order number. Most refunds run within 24 hours, land within 3 to 7 business days.
