Do I need an account to buy?+

No. Your receipt and download links email to you. An account is optional and only helps with re-downloading later.

What file formats do I get?+

PDF for printing. PNG for slides and apps. Both ship in every paid product. Free samples are PDF only.

Can I share these with my class?+

Yes, within a single classroom. Multi-classroom and district licenses are at the bottom of every product page.

What is your refund policy?+

If the file does not work, I will refund you. Digital files are exempt from blanket refunds, but I want you happy with what you bought.

How long does the download link last?+

A signed link expires after 24 hours. Re-issue any time from your library, or use the recover-a-receipt page.
